Privacy Policy

Your dance floor stays private.

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Applies to Pocket Ballroom for Android, Pocket Ballroom for Wear OS, and pocketballroom.com.

The Short Version: Pocket Ballroom is built offline-first and privacy-first. When you tap Listen, microphone audio is analyzed in volatile memory by our on-device neural rhythm and style models—raw room audio is never recorded, saved, or uploaded. When online, song identification uses compact one-way mathematical signatures via Apple ShazamKit. We do not sell personal data, run ad trackers, or request Notification Access.

1. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Pocket Ballroom requests microphone access solely when you actively start listening to music on your phone or Wear OS watch so our on-device rhythm and style engines (Beat This! and BallroomStyleNet) can estimate tempo (BPM), time signature (3/4 vs. 4/4), musical groove, and Beat 1 timing.

  • All rhythm, Beat 1, and style analysis happens 100% locally on your device in temporary RAM.
  • When you tap Listen on a paired Wear OS watch, a brief 6-second open-air audio clip is streamed strictly point-to-point over local Bluetooth (Wear OS ChannelClient) to your own paired phone in volatile RAM for local neural analysis and one-way ShazamKit signature lookup—never written to disk and never uploaded as raw audio.
  • Raw room audio is never written to disk, never recorded, and never uploaded to Pocket Ballroom servers or third parties.
  • You can stop listening at any time with a single tap, immediately releasing the microphone.

2. Parallel Song Identification (Apple ShazamKit)

When an internet connection is available during active listening, Pocket Ballroom generates a compact, one-way mathematical audio signature locally on your device using Apple ShazamKit to match the song title and artist.

  • Raw audio never leaves your device, and original audio or speech cannot be reconstructed from a one-way ShazamKit signature.
  • Matched song titles are resolved locally against the bundled 18,090-song offline dance catalog and your personal Studio Memory on your phone.
  • When your phone is offline (such as in a basement studio without Wi-Fi), song lookup is skipped automatically and identification runs 100% on-device with zero network requests.

3. Minimal Permissions

The Android phone app requests only three permissions: RECORD_AUDIO (active listening), VIBRATE (optional Beat Bar preview haptics), and INTERNET (optional online ShazamKit signature lookup and YouTube links you choose to open). Pocket Ballroom does not request Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE), Location, Contacts, Camera, or background always-on microphone recording.

The Wear OS watch app additionally uses standard Wear OS foreground service, wake-lock, and deferred ongoing-activity notification permissions solely to keep the Haptic Beat Coach accurate while your wrist screen dims between songs.

4. Local Data Stored on Your Device

Your personal dance repertoire—including moves marked ✓ Know, pinned ★ Tonight steps, custom studio nicknames, bookmarked studio songs and their local on-device acoustic fingerprints, Timing Sync haptic offset, Leader/Follower role preference, and links to local class videos you attach—is stored locally on your device in Android Room and DataStore and synced only between your own paired phone and Wear OS watch via Google Play Services Wearable APIs.

5. Optional User-Initiated External Actions

Certain optional actions only occur when you explicitly initiate them:

  • Music App Share & YouTube Clips: Sharing a track into Pocket Ballroom from Spotify, YouTube, Shazam, or Google Song Search—or tapping a saved YouTube demo link—uses standard Android intents handled by those apps under their respective privacy policies.

6. Website & Waitlist Communications

On pocketballroom.com, we do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts. If you voluntarily submit your email for Android Beta, iOS Waitlist, or Studio Instructor updates, we use your email solely to send the updates you requested. You may request deletion of your email at any time by writing to privacy@pocketballroom.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Pocket Ballroom is designed for general adult and teen social ballroom dancers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes & Contact

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at privacy@pocketballroom.com.
