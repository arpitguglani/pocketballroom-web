Privacy Policy

Your dance floor stays private.

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Applies to Pocket Ballroom for Android, Pocket Ballroom for Wear OS, and pocketballroom.com.

The Short Version: Pocket Ballroom is built offline-first. When you tap Listen, microphone audio is analyzed strictly in temporary memory on your device to estimate BPM, meter, and Beat 1. We never record, store, or upload your room audio, and we do not sell personal data or run third-party ad trackers.

1. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Pocket Ballroom requests microphone access solely when you actively start listening to music on your phone or Wear OS watch so the on-device rhythm engine can estimate tempo (BPM), time signature (3/4 vs. 4/4), musical feel, and Beat 1 timing.

  • All audio signal processing happens 100% locally on your device in volatile RAM.
  • Raw audio is never written to disk, never saved, and never transmitted to Pocket Ballroom servers or third parties during standard offline listening.
  • You can stop listening at any time with a single tap, immediately releasing the microphone.

2. Optional Notification Access (Android “Now Playing”)

On compatible Android devices (such as Google Pixel phones with offline Now Playing), you may optionally grant Notification Access in Android Settings so Pocket Ballroom can automatically read the song title and artist displayed by the system Now Playing notification and map it to a ballroom dance.

  • Pocket Ballroom filters strictly for music/Now Playing notifications and ignores personal messages, emails, and other app notifications.
  • Recognized song titles are matched locally against your on-device song catalog and Studio DJ Memory and are never uploaded off your device.
  • You can revoke Notification Access at any time in your Android system settings.

3. Local Data Stored on Your Device

Your personal dance repertoire—including moves marked ✓ Know, pinned ★ Tonight steps, custom studio nicknames, Studio DJ Memory song overrides, Leader/Follower role preference, and links to local class videos you attach—is stored locally on your device using Android’s Room database and synced only between your own paired phone and Wear OS watch via Google Play Services Wearable APIs.

4. Optional User-Initiated External Actions

Certain optional actions only occur when you explicitly tap them:

  • YouTube / Music App Links: Tapping a saved YouTube demo link or sharing a track from Spotify/Shazam opens or receives standard Android intents handled by those apps under their respective privacy policies.
  • Opt-In Cloud Assist (if enabled in Settings): If you explicitly enable an optional cloud-assisted identification feature in Settings using your own configuration, only that specific user-triggered request is sent over HTTPS. It is disabled by default.

5. Website & Waitlist Communications

On pocketballroom.com, we do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts. If you voluntarily submit your email for Android Beta, iOS Waitlist, or Studio Instructor updates, we use your email solely to send the updates you requested. You may request deletion of your email at any time by writing to privacy@pocketballroom.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

Pocket Ballroom is designed for general adult and teen social ballroom dancers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes & Contact

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at privacy@pocketballroom.com.
