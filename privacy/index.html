Privacy Policy

Your dance floor stays private.

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Applies to Pocket Ballroom for Android, Pocket Ballroom for Wear OS, and pocketballroom.com.

The Short Version: Pocket Ballroom is built offline-first with minimal permissions (RECORD_AUDIO, VIBRATE, and INTERNET—no Notification Access, no account required, no ad trackers). When you tap Listen, raw room audio is never recorded, saved, or uploaded. On-device AI analyzes rhythm locally in memory, and optional online song matching sends only a non-reversible one-way acoustic signature.

1. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO) & On-Device Audio AI

Pocket Ballroom requests microphone access solely when you actively start listening to music on your phone or Wear OS watch so our on-device neural rhythm and style models can estimate tempo (BPM), time signature (3/4 vs. 4/4), ballroom style, and Beat 1 timing.

  • All rhythm, beat, and style inference runs 100% locally on your device in volatile RAM.
  • Raw room audio is never written to disk, never saved, and never uploaded to Pocket Ballroom servers or any third party.
  • You can stop listening at any time with a single tap, immediately releasing the microphone.

2. Privacy-Safe Song Identification (Apple ShazamKit)

When your device has an internet connection and you tap Listen, Pocket Ballroom may convert a brief snippet of the live audio stream on-device into a non-reversible, one-way mathematical acoustic signature (via Apple’s ShazamKit SDK) and send only that signature over HTTPS to identify the song title and artist.

  • Raw audio and voices cannot be reconstructed from a one-way acoustic signature.
  • Matched song titles are looked up locally against Pocket Ballroom’s bundled 18,090-song offline dance catalog and your personal Studio Memory.
  • When offline (no studio Wi-Fi or cell signal), Pocket Ballroom automatically uses its 100% on-device rhythm, style, and local fingerprint engines with zero network traffic.

3. Local Data Stored on Your Device

Your personal dance repertoire—including moves marked ✓ Know, pinned ★ Tonight steps, custom studio nicknames, bookmarked Studio Memory song overrides (and any local acoustic fingerprints for untitled tracks you bookmark), Leader/Follower role preference, and links to local class videos you attach—is stored strictly on your device in Android’s Room database and synced only between your own paired phone and Wear OS watch via Google Play Services Wearable APIs.

4. Permissions We Do Not Request

Pocket Ballroom does not request Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE), Location, Contacts, Camera, or background always-on microphone recording.

5. Optional User-Initiated External Links

Tapping a saved YouTube demo link or sharing a track from Spotify, YouTube, or Shazam uses standard Android intents handled by those apps under their respective privacy policies.

6. Website & Waitlist Communications

On pocketballroom.com, we do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts. If you voluntarily submit your email for Android Beta, iOS Waitlist, or Studio Instructor updates, we use your email solely to send the updates you requested. You may request deletion of your email at any time by writing to privacy@pocketballroom.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Pocket Ballroom is designed for general adult and teen social ballroom dancers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes & Contact

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at privacy@pocketballroom.com.
