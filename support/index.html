Support & Troubleshooting

Here to help you before Friday’s social.

Quick setup tips for Android, Google Pixel Now Playing, and Wear OS—or email us directly at hello@pocketballroom.com.

Enabling Automatic Pixel “Now Playing”

On Google Pixel phones, Pocket Ballroom can identify the song playing in your studio with zero taps:

  • Open Android Settings → Sound & vibration → Now Playing and make sure Identify songs playing nearby is enabled.
  • Inside Pocket Ballroom, open Settings → Enable Now Playing Auto-Reader and grant Notification Access.
  • Whenever Pixel recognizes a track, your Party Card updates automatically with the best matching dance and your known steps.

Sharing Songs from Shazam, Spotify, or YouTube

On any Android phone, tap Share → Pocket Ballroom from Shazam, Google “Search a song,” Spotify, or YouTube. Pocket Ballroom maps the song to Primary and Secondary dances and lets you save a custom override with Remember for My Studio DJ.

Pairing Your Wear OS Haptic Beat Coach

  • Install Pocket Ballroom on both your Android phone and your Wear OS 3+ watch.
  • Add the Pocket Ballroom Quick Tile to your watch carousel for 1-swipe access between songs.
  • During a song, tap once anywhere on the watch dial to snap Beat 1 without looking down, or rotate the crown/bezel to nudge BPM.

Contact & Feedback
