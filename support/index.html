Support & Troubleshooting

Here to help you before Friday’s social.

Quick setup tips for online & offline dance ID, My Moves, and Wear OS Beat Coach—or email us directly at hello@pocketballroom.com.

1. Identifying a Dance (Online & Offline)

Tap the Champagne Gold Listen orb when a song starts. Pocket Ballroom locks in ~5–7 seconds on every Android phone with no special permissions required:

  • Online + Offline Hybrid: When connected, parallel Apple ShazamKit identifies the track via privacy-safe one-way signatures and looks it up in the bundled 18,090-song catalog (with live BPM/meter checks for studio covers). When offline, on-device Beat This! and BallroomStyleNet neural models detect tempo, meter, Beat 1, and groove automatically.
  • Quick Refinements: Tap any alternative dance pill (or a gold ★ pill for a dance you already know) to switch instantly, or open Adjust ▾ to flip Half speed / Double speed or pick a Feel chip.
  • Bookmark for My Studio: Tap the Bookmark icon on any result card—even for untitled offline songs—so Pocket Ballroom remembers your preferred dance next Friday.

2. Searching 18,090 Songs or Sharing from Music Apps

Tap the top-right Search icon on the Listen screen to search all 18,090 cataloged songs and your Studio Memory anytime—or tap Share → Pocket Ballroom from Spotify, YouTube, Shazam, or Google Song Search.

3. Managing Your Steps & Muted Class Clips

  • Open the My Moves tab to toggle between Leader and Follower cues, mark moves ✓ Know, pin tonight’s group class pattern with ★ Tonight, or add your instructor’s custom nickname.
  • Tap any step to attach a short phone video from class (loops inline and muted by default at 0.5x / 0.75x / 1.0x) or link a timestamped YouTube recap.
  • On the Listen screen, tap Practice a rhythm at the bottom to rehearse any of the 9 dances at home.

4. Pairing Wear OS Haptic Beat Coach & Timing Sync

  • Install Pocket Ballroom on both your Android phone and your Wear OS 3+ watch.
  • Wrist-Mic Listen (Phone Locked in Pocket): Tap Listen on your watch to record clean 6-second open-air audio on your wrist mic and run your paired phone’s full ShazamKit + neural AI + 18,090-song catalog engine over Bluetooth—even while your phone stays locked in your pocket. If your phone isn’t nearby, your watch seamlessly falls back to standalone rhythm detection on that clip.
  • 3-Page Swipeable Live Session: Stay on the center Beat Ring for giant blind-tap Beat-1 snap and rotary crown ±1 BPM; swipe left for Controls (Next Song, ±1 BPM, Auto-Fade, alternate dances); swipe right for your Moves. Every watch tap syncs bidirectionally with your phone.
  • Live-Aware Quick Tile & Timing Sync: Add the live-aware Pocket Ballroom Quick Tile (Listen | Moves when idle; Dance · BPM + Open | Stop when active), and open Settings → Watch & Beat Coach → Timing Sync (120 BPM click + offset slider) to fine-tune wrist vibration timing if needed.

5. Contact & Feedback

Have a song your studio DJ plays every Friday, a feature idea, or a bug report? Write to us anytime at hello@pocketballroom.com. We typically reply within 1–2 business days.
