Support & Troubleshooting

Here to help you before Friday’s social.

Quick setup tips for Android and Wear OS—or email us directly at hello@pocketballroom.com.

How Song Identification & Offline Mode Work

Tap Listen on your phone (or right from your Wear OS watch) when a song starts:

  • When online: Pocket Ballroom generates a privacy-safe one-way acoustic signature locally to identify the song in ~2 seconds, then maps it against our bundled 18,090-song offline ballroom catalog while locking onto live Beat 1.
  • When offline (no studio Wi-Fi): On-device neural rhythm & style AI analyzes live BPM, 3/4 vs. 4/4 meter, musical feel, and Beat 1 completely offline.
  • Bookmark for Studio Memory: Tap the bookmark icon on any result to remember your studio’s exact dance for that track—even untitled offline tracks.
  • Share from Music Apps: Tap Share → Pocket Ballroom from Spotify, YouTube, Shazam, or Google Song Search on any Android phone.

Using Your Wear OS Haptic Beat Coach

  • Install Pocket Ballroom on both your Android phone and your Wear OS 3+ watch.
  • Phone-First Remote Listen: Tap Listen on your wrist to trigger your paired phone’s full song & rhythm engine (no watch mic prompt needed) and stream the locked dance + Beat 1 straight back to your watch. If your phone isn’t nearby, your watch seamlessly listens on its own.
  • 3-Page Live Session: Stay on the center Beat Ring for blind 1-tap Beat-1 snap and rotary crown ±1 BPM; swipe left for Controls (Next Song, Auto-Fade, alternate dances); swipe right for your Moves.
  • Add the live-aware Pocket Ballroom Quick Tile for 1-swipe access between dances.

Fine-Tuning Wrist Beat Timing (Timing Sync)

If your watch buzzes slightly ahead of or behind the music on your specific phone/watch pair, open Settings → Watch & Beat Coach → Timing Sync to tap along with a calm 120 BPM click and dial in your exact offset in seconds.
