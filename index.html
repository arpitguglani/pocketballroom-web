The 5-Second Floor Rule

Turn sideline hesitation into confident first steps.

A song starts in a dim studio, experienced dancers glide onto the floor, and every newcomer asks the same three silent questions. Pocket Ballroom answers all three without breaking the ambiance.

01 · LISTEN

“What dance is this?”

On-device rhythm AI listens to the room for BPM, 3/4 vs. 4/4 meter, and groove—paired with automatic Android Now Playing detection and 1-tap Share from your favorite music apps.

02 · GLANCE

“Which moves do I know?”

Skip 500-move syllabi. Your Party Card surfaces only the 5–6 steps you actually know—with Leader or Follower cues, your instructor’s nicknames, and muted slow-mo class clips.

03 · STEP ON

“Where is Beat 1?”

Feel the downbeat silently on your Wear OS wrist—tuned to real dance timing like Slow–Quick–Quick and 2–3–Cha-Cha-Cha, with zero beeps and pure OLED black.

01 · Instant Dance ID

Know the dance—and teach it your studio DJ’s playlist.

Pop-crossover tracks around 120 BPM can fit Cha-Cha, Foxtrot, Rumba, or Tango depending on the feel. Tap a single Feel Chip to settle close calls, or save a track once with Studio DJ Memory so it locks automatically next Friday.

  • Curated Party Catalog: Ships with hundreds of verified ballroom & pop-crossover tracks mapped to Primary and Secondary dances.
  • Studio DJ Memory: Tap Remember for My Studio DJ to store your studio’s exact song-to-dance overrides locally on your phone.
  • Zero-Tap & Share Support: Works automatically with Pixel Now Playing or via Android Share from Spotify, YouTube, or Shazam.

03 · Wear OS Haptic Beat Coach

Feel Beat 1 silently on your wrist.

Leave your phone in your pocket. Your Wear OS watch locks onto the song’s live BPM and pulses real dance-step timing directly to your wrist on a zero-glare pure OLED black canvas.

  • Three Tactile Modes: Switch between Buzz: Steps (Slow–Quick–Quick), Buzz: Beats, or Buzz: Beat 1.
  • Training Wheels Auto-Fade: Gradually fades haptics after 4–8 bars once you’ve locked into the groove on your own.
  • Blind 1-Tap Beat 1 Snap & Quick Tile: Re-align Beat 1 without looking down, nudge BPM with the crown, or launch in one swipe.
