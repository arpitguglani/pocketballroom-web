Questions

Good to know before Friday night.

Do I need a smartwatch?
No. The Android app works completely on its own—identifying the dance, showing your steps, and pulsing the Beat Bar. A Wear OS watch simply adds silent wrist haptics and Wrist Listen.
Does it work without studio Wi-Fi?
Yes. Songs, rhythm detection, your steps, class videos, and Bluetooth watch sync all work offline in basement studios.
Does it record my room audio?
Never. Audio is analyzed in temporary memory and discarded immediately—never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
Is it coming to iPhone and Apple Watch?
Yes—iOS and Apple Watch are next. Join the waitlist below for an early TestFlight invite.
