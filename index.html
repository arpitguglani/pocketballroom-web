Three Questions · Five Seconds

Turn sideline hesitation into confident first steps.

A song starts in a dim studio and experienced dancers glide onto the floor. Pocket Ballroom answers the three questions every social dancer asks—calmly, accurately, and without breaking the room.

01 · WHAT’S THE DANCE?

“What dance is this song?”

Parallel Apple ShazamKit (100M+ tracks via privacy-safe one-way signatures) matched to an 18,090-song offline catalog, backed by two on-device neural networks (Beat This! + BallroomStyleNet) that read tempo, 3/4 vs. 4/4 meter, and groove even completely offline.

02 · WHAT ARE THE STEPS?

“Which moves do I actually know?”

Skip 500-move syllabi. Your Party Card puts only the 5–6 steps you know right above the fold—with Leader or Follower cues, ★ Tonight class pins, custom instructor nicknames, and muted slow-mo class clips.

03 · WHAT’S THE BEAT?

“Where is Beat 1?”

Tap Listen right from your Wear OS wrist (even with your phone locked in your pocket) or follow the Phone Beat Bar to feel Beat 1 silently—pulsing real dance timing like Slow–Quick–Quick and 2–3–Cha-Cha-Cha on pure OLED black.

03 · What’s the Beat

Questions

Everything you want to know before Friday night.

Do I need a Wear OS smartwatch to use Pocket Ballroom?
Not at all. The Android phone app works completely standalone with instant Song & Dance ID, quiet [Adjust ▾] refinements, Studio Memory bookmarks, your Leader/Follower steps, the docked Beat Bar, and muted slow-mo class clips. Pairing a Wear OS 3+ watch adds wrist-mic Listen (even with your phone locked in your pocket), the 3-page swipeable live session, silent wrist haptics, bidirectional sync, and the live-aware Quick Tile.
Does it really work without Wi-Fi or cell service in a studio?
Yes. When you’re offline, our dual on-device neural networks (Beat This! + BallroomStyleNet), the 18,090-song catalog, your bookmarked studio tracks, your known steps, and local class videos all run 100% on your phone—phone-to-watch sync works over Bluetooth with zero internet, and even untethered watch-initiated Listen falls back seamlessly to standalone on-watch rhythm analysis on the exact clip already captured.
How does song identification work—and does it record my room audio?
When you tap Listen, Pocket Ballroom locks in ~5–7 seconds. Online, it generates a one-way mathematical audio signature locally via Apple ShazamKit (so raw room audio never leaves your phone) and matches against 18,090 ballroom & pop-crossover songs with live BPM/meter cover protection. Offline, our on-device neural beat and style models take over seamlessly. You can also bookmark any song—even untitled offline tracks—or tap Share → Pocket Ballroom from Spotify, YouTube, or Shazam, with zero Notification Access required.
What if a 120 BPM pop track could be Rumba, Cha-Cha, or Foxtrot?
Pocket Ballroom uses BallroomStyleNet groove AI plus calibrated confidence (showing Likely instead of pretending to be 95% sure on close calls), highlights an alternative dance you already know with a gold ★ pill, and tucks Half speed, Double speed, and Feel chips cleanly inside [Adjust ▾]. Bookmark your choice once and it locks automatically next Friday.
Is Pocket Ballroom coming to iOS and Apple Watch?
Yes—iOS and Apple Watch are next on our roadmap, bringing the same Midnight Ballroom design, on-device rhythm ID, and silent wrist haptics via Apple Watch. Join the iOS Waitlist above to get an early TestFlight invite.
